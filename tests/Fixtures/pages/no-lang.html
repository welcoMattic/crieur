A short guide to winter cycling

Cycling through the winter is easier than most people think. The most important rule is to be visible. The days are short, so use a white light at the front and a red light at the back, even in the afternoon.

Next, protect your hands and your ears. Cold fingers make it hard to use the brakes, and a thin hat under the helmet keeps the wind away. Wear layers that you can open when you get warm.

Look after the bike

Salt and wet roads damage the chain. Clean it once a week and add a little oil. Check the tyre pressure often, because cold air lowers it. A slightly lower pressure gives a better grip on wet streets.
