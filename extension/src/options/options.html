Connect to your Crieur

Crieur reads the article of the page you are viewing and sends it to your own Crieur server, which reads it aloud.

The address you open Crieur with. The browser asks once for the right to reach it.

The value of CRIEUR_API_TOKEN on the server. Kept in this browser only.

What the extension does with your pages

It reads a page only when you ask it to: the toolbar button, its keyboard shortcut or the menu of the page.

It sends the article and its address to the server above, and nowhere else. No cookie, no browsing history, no analytics.

It reads what your browser already shows you: it never gets around a login or a subscription.
